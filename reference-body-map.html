Try the Demo

The Problem

Many students struggle with maintaining consistent study habits. Distractions, lack of motivation, and poor tracking lead to inconsistent progress, missed deadlines, and burnout. Without a simple way to visualize and reward daily effort, building streaks becomes nearly impossible.

Our Solution

StudyStreak is a user-friendly web app that gamifies studying by tracking daily streaks. Log your study sessions, view your progress, and stay motivated with visual rewards. It's designed for students, by students, to make consistency fun and achievable.

Features

Streak Tracking

Monitor your daily study streaks and never lose momentum.

Progress Visualization

View charts and stats to see your improvement over time.

Reminders

Get gentle notifications to keep your study routine on track.

Mobile Friendly

Access your streaks anywhere with a responsive design.

Tech Stack

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Storage: LocalStorage for client-side data
  • Design: Responsive with dark theme and gradients

How It Works

  1. Sign up and set your study goals.
  2. Log each study session daily.
  3. Watch your streak grow and earn badges.
  4. Stay motivated with progress insights.
